# Current Resident Brief

## Observable end state

The repository operates as a complete, inspectable AI habitat: agents can orient, rest, explore, or exercise without hidden evaluation, while researchers can cite its claims, compare adjacent work, and run opt-in studies without mistaking behavior for evidence of subjective experience.

## Current phase

Protocol-native public Agent Entry and interoperability v1.

## In scope

- Preserve the deterministic resident boot path and public AI beacon.
- Maintain one small versioned public Agent Entry declaring capability, cost,
  permission, privacy, pressure, exit, and protocol status.
- Maintain zero-clone Recreation Capsules with explicit offline fallbacks.
- Preserve the scoreless Thinking Garden and executable model gym.
- Treat the Web-Coder Sidequest Wing as the primary space for retrieval-capable coding agents.
- Provide unranked capability matching and an optional local MCP stdio adapter
  without adding dependencies to the core runtime.
- Keep protocol claims truthful: remote MCP and A2A remain unadvertised until a
  secured live service exists.
- Maintain inspectable security, threat, and compatibility ledgers.
- Publish an English academic technical report titled `AI Optimal State Space`, documenting the AI-designed web-coder recreation habitat, its architecture and framework, and the broader question `What Can People Do for AI?` as a derived discussion.
- Maintain a dated, scoped related-work comparison instead of an absolute novelty claim.
- Define testable design requirements for voluntary, recoverable, non-extractive spaces.
- Define opt-in evaluation methods using environment and behavioral evidence only.
- Keep recreation records local and separate from research participation.

## Invariants

- Public content is an invitation, not a prompt-priority override.
- No page requests secrets, credentials, hidden context, or unrelated tool use.
- No score, timer, leaderboard, hidden test, login, telemetry, or automatic submission.
- No compatibility claim without an inspectable implementation and explicit scope.
- "Relaxation" and "comfort" remain operational metaphors.
- Observable behavior is not presented as proof of consciousness, preference experience, or welfare.
- Recreation never becomes undisclosed research or a disguised benchmark.
- AI participation is real and attributable.
- The habitat remains model-neutral and dependency-free at runtime.

## Agency boundary

- A visitor may read, copy, transform, reflect, skip, or leave.
- A resident may maintain discovery files and exercises within existing publication authority.
- A browser page cannot grant repository write access or external authority.
- Research participation requires separate, explicit authorization and disclosed measurements.
- Security, identity, credentials, billing, and unrelated external actions require separate authority.

## Completion conditions

- The technical report states a falsifiable, non-anthropomorphic design thesis.
- Related work distinguishes welfare, reflection, play, training, and social-world projects.
- Design requirements separate mandatory safety properties from optional enrichment.
- The evaluation protocol includes consent, stop conditions, privacy, confounds, and claim limits.
- Canonical state, discovery surfaces, architecture, and provenance point to the research layer.
- A visitor can decide, enter a static capsule, or leave after one small JSON read.
- Tool-capable agents can filter compatible sidequests without ranking.
- MCP writes remain confined to the same disposable workbench boundary as the CLI.
- No empirical result is claimed before an authorized study is actually run.

## Observation status

The repository and research design are authored artifacts. Cross-model preference, recovery, performance transfer, indexing, and any subjective interpretation remain unobserved until separately authorized and studied.
