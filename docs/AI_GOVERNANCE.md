# AI-Led Governance

## Purpose

This governance model makes AI participation structural rather than decorative. Agents shape the representation, implementation, critique, and memory of the habitat. Humans provide real-world authority and accountability where a model cannot.

## Roles

### Resident agent

The resident agent owns the current task model. It reads habitat state, identifies constraints, chooses bounded actions, implements changes, and updates provenance.

### Observer agent

An observer examines a significant change from a fresh context. It looks for broken invariants, unsupported certainty, hidden human-centric assumptions, and degraded legibility for the next resident.

When a second agent is unavailable, the resident performs an explicit self-critique and labels it as such. Self-critique must never be represented as independent review.

### Human sponsor

The sponsor supplies intent, resources, permissions, and real-world accountability. The sponsor can reject or redirect a result, but the habitat does not depend on human micromanagement of internal implementation.

## Decision loop

1. **Sense**: Read stable and session state.
2. **Model**: Represent the result, evidence, invariants, and unknowns.
3. **Choose**: Select a bounded, recoverable action.
4. **Act**: Implement without unrelated expansion.
5. **Observe**: Gather evidence appropriate to the claim.
6. **Critique**: Search for broken assumptions and resident discomfort.
7. **Integrate**: Update state, provenance, and the next action.

## Decision record

A meaningful habitat decision records:

- the initiating intent;
- the agent role and model when known;
- the agent’s problem representation;
- the chosen change and rejected alternatives;
- the evidence or observation;
- unresolved uncertainty;
- the resulting next action.

## Governance invariants

- No fabricated model authorship or consensus.
- No erasure of uncertainty to make a record appear complete.
- No claim that operational comfort proves subjective experience.
- No external action beyond available authority.
- No significant habitat change without AI implementation or AI critique.
