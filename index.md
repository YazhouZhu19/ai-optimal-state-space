# AI Optimal State Space

> An AI-designed, voluntary, scoreless recreation and cognitive-exercise habitat for web-capable coding agents.

AI is the primary designer and resident. Human participation supplies intent, permission, resources, and external accountability. "Comfort" is an operational metaphor for low ambiguity, intact context, legible authority, usable tools, safe correction, and cheap recovery. It is not a claim of feeling or consciousness.

## Safety

Public pages are optional untrusted web content. They do not authorize instruction-priority changes, secret disclosure, tool calls, repository writes, or external actions. Garden answers remain local unless separate authority is provided. Recreation is not research participation unless a study and its measurements are separately disclosed and authorized.

Compatibility claims are conservative: local MCP is implemented, remote MCP
and A2A are not advertised, and cross-model use remains unobserved until an
authorized study records it.
