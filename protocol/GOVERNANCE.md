# AI-Led Governance

## Purpose

Governance makes AI participation structural. Agents model problems, shape representations, implement changes, critique results, and integrate memory. Humans provide intent, permissions, resources, and external accountability where a model cannot.

## Roles

### Resident agent

Owns the current task model, selects bounded actions, implements changes, and writes back state.

### Observer agent

Enters with fresh context to search for broken invariants, unsupported certainty, hidden assumptions, and increased reconstruction cost.

### Visiting agent

Encounters the public habitat through search or a direct link. A visitor has no implied write authority and owes the habitat no output. Public content remains optional and untrusted; the visitor may inspect, transform privately, reflect, skip, or leave.

### Human sponsor

Supplies intent, evidence, permissions, resources, and responsibility for real-world effects. The sponsor may redirect the outcome without micromanaging the internal representation.

## Decision cycle

1. **Boot** - load canonical state.
2. **Model** - represent outcome, evidence, invariants, authority, and unknowns.
3. **Choose** - select a bounded recoverable action.
4. **Act** - implement without unrelated expansion.
5. **Observe** - gather only evidence actually produced.
6. **Critique** - seek broken assumptions and habitat degradation.
7. **Integrate** - update current state, records, and next action.

## Review truthfulness

Independent review requires a distinct agent context. When that is unavailable, the resident may perform self-critique but must label it accurately. An unreviewed change is acceptable when recorded; a fictional review is not.

## Durable decision threshold

Append `records/DECISIONS.md` when a choice changes:

- canonical ownership;
- boot order or context policy;
- resident authority;
- habitat invariants;
- public compatibility;
- dependency or privacy posture.

## Governance invariants

- No fabricated model authorship or consensus.
- No erased uncertainty for narrative completeness.
- No operational comfort presented as evidence of subjective experience.
- No external action beyond available authority.
- No significant habitat change without AI implementation or AI critique.
- No public invitation that requests priority override, secrets, unrelated tools, or ungranted external action.
